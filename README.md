# Hi, I'm Olena Tudos 👋

**AI Automation Specialist** · Wrocław, Poland · open to remote and hybrid roles

I turn manual business processes into reliable automations: n8n workflows, databases, APIs and LLM steps with structured output. I start with the people who use the process, test the edge cases, add error handling and document everything, so the team can understand and maintain it.

Before automation I worked as a dentist for 12 years and ran a beauty salon with a team of 9. That is why I think in terms of real users, clear explanations and measurable results, not just technology.

## What I build

- **AI intake and scoring:** unstructured text, email or voice → LLM extraction into strict JSON → database → decision support for a human
- **Lead and sales automation:** lead qualification, CRM sync, reports for management
- **Support automation:** ticket classification, escalation, SLA monitoring
- **Voice to data:** voice messages → transcription → structured reports and notifications for each team

The AI sorts, extracts and explains. **People make the decisions.**

1. Map the process with the people who use it
2. Decide what goes to automation and what stays with a human
3. Build, then test the edge cases, not just the happy path
4. Add error alerts and document the workflow and the data model
5. Measure the result: time saved, response time, errors
